The opportunity

Bridging the deep-tech gap

Across West Africa, technical talent is underused because skill building and industrialization are disconnected. Consumer software and payments have scaled, yet the region still imports almost all of its hardware, industrial control systems and AI data infrastructure.

Human capital development

With academic partners like Yabatech, we recruit, train and deploy elite engineering talent on real-world software and hardware projects.

Solving global data bottlenecks

Proprietary hardware-software systems, such as our egocentric robotics data engines, supply high-value data to global AI and robotics markets.

Enforcing national software standards

An independent testing and quality assurance body for software reliability under national regulatory frameworks.
